4. Optional Google sign-in and cloud saves

When you use these features, Google and we process authentication credentials, an account identifier, an email address and profile information supplied by Google where applicable, IP addresses and technical connection information. Android settings also allow resetting or deleting the advertising ID; this does not replace withdrawing consent in the form.
